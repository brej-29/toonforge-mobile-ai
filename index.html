Good ideas shouldn't
need an upload.

A classroom handout. A zine about your neighbourhood. A print of a favourite place. Sometimes you need a simpler image, without a whole design suite.

Toonforge keeps the photo and the work on your device. It simplifies texture and draws outlines with predictable image filters. It doesn't invent details, and it isn't an anonymity tool.

Read the evidence & limitations ↗

Less mystery. More making.

How does it work?

An edge-aware filter smooths similar neighbouring colours. Tone quantization simplifies shading, then a Sobel edge map adds outlines. A background worker keeps the controls responsive. Every image is processed locally.

What do the live numbers mean?

Processing is the filter's elapsed time on your device, excluding decode and export. Image darkness is mean pixel darkness, not printer toner usage. Compare styles to see how your image changes. The upload count describes this editor's image workflow; your host still receives ordinary page requests.

What has actually been tested?

Controlled synthetic fixtures test texture suppression and boundary retention against simple posterization. They measure engineering behaviour, not whether people prefer the artwork. The full plan and reproducible results are in the repository.

Inspect the evaluation ↗
What stays on my device?

Your selected photo, processed pixels, and exports stay in browser memory. There is no photo server, analytics, or account. Clearing removes the active photo from the editor; downloaded files remain on your device. The app caches its public assets for offline use after the first visit.
